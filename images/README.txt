Put your figure image files in this folder.

Requirements from the Guidelines page of the preliminary website:
- At least 3 and no more than 5 figures across the whole site.
- Every figure needs a caption and a source.
- Images must be copyright-clear: Creative Commons, public domain, or used
  with permission. Failing this costs marks.

To place an image, replace the dashed "figure-slot" block on the page with:

  <img src="../images/your-file.png" alt="Description of the image">

(On index.html the path is images/your-file.png, without the "../".)
